How not to play

Everyone in the waiting room is earning Disinterest. Finish with the most by being as uninvolved as possible until you leave. If Excitement fills up, Undertime ends the match early.

WASD
Shuffle around. On a phone, drag the left side of the room.
The Point
A dashed zone that moves every 18 seconds. Stay out of it. Standing in it means you're involved, which raises Excitement.
Stillness
Stand still for a second and your Disinterest doubles.
Their glances
Pale wedges show where other people are looking. Linger in one and they'll notice you, which costs you Disinterest and might start small talk.
Your glance
You have one too, in your colour. Keep someone in it for a moment and you notice them, which costs them 15 Disinterest. If they're looking back at you when it happens, that's eye contact, and everybody loses. On a computer you face your cursor while standing still.
Space
Warmup. Stand still for 3 seconds, then nothing happens. That's worth +15.
E
Penultimate. Nobody can notice you for a while. It starts at its best and gets weaker with every use and every second.
Q
Unping. Hide the Point from yourself for 7 seconds. Disinterest ×1.5 while you don't know where it is.
Click
Toss a receipt. Land it close to someone's shoes without hitting them for bonus points. On a phone, tap the right side.
Courtesy
The opposite of shields. It protects other people from you when you bump into them.
Other players
In a room with friends, everyone shares the Point and the Excitement meter, and you notice each other the same way you notice anyone else. If Excitement fills up, Undertime ends it for everybody.
Everyone else
The other people in the room are played by the computer, usually as the Zeroes nobody has. They follow the same rules: they stay out of the Point, stand still, do their own Warmups, and try to notice whoever is ahead. They can only dodge your glance if they can see you looking, so come at them from behind.
Rooms
Each match is at the DMV, a dentist’s office or a laundromat. Tall things like kiosks, fish tanks and washing machines block glances, yours included.
Now serving
Every so often the desk calls a number. If it’s yours, don’t get noticed for 7 seconds and you get +40. Everyone else starts looking around, and anyone moving looks suspicious.
A phone rings
Everyone turns to look at it. Don’t stand near it. With everyone facing the phone, it’s a good moment to notice people from behind.
Lights out
For 6 seconds glances are much shorter and everyone earns 1.5×.

The camera is fourth-person: you're watching from below a translucent floor. Those shapes are shoe soles. Your rank goes down as you get better, and your Zero's colour wears off the more you play.
